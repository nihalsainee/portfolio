Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what most clients ask before booking a project.

How long does a website take to build?
A Basic Website typically takes 5–7 days, a Professional Website 2–3 weeks, and an E-Commerce site 3–4 weeks, depending on content and revisions.
Do you work with clients outside India?
Yes — I work with clients globally. All communication, proposals, and payments are handled remotely over WhatsApp, email, and video calls.
What if my project doesn't fit a package?
Select "Custom Project" in the booking form, or click "Get a Custom Quote" in the Services section. I'll review your requirements and send a tailored proposal.
Do you offer support after the website is live?
Every package includes free support for 30–90 days. After that, the Maintenance & Hosting plan keeps your site updated, backed up, and monitored for a flat monthly fee.
How do I pay for a project?
Typically a deposit to begin work and the balance on delivery, shared with you in the proposal. Payment details are confirmed directly over WhatsApp or email.
